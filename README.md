🚀 Excited to share my latest Data Analytics Project: Ride-Booking Operational & Revenue Dashboard!

Understanding operational bottlenecks and revenue drivers is key for urban mobility platforms. Using a dataset of 150,000+ ride bookings, I built an end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze ride success rates, cancellation drivers, and operational turnaround times.

📊 Key Insights Uncovered:
Overall Completion: Out of 150K total bookings, 62% (93K rides) were completed, generating ₹47M+ in completed revenue (out of ₹52M total booking value).

Cancellation Trends: 18% of rides were cancelled by drivers, while 7% were cancelled by customers. Top driver cancellation factors included customer-related issues and capacity limits, while customer cancellations peaked due to wrong addresses or driver delays.

Vehicle & Payment Dynamics: Auto and Go Mini dominated total ride volume and revenue, with UPI emerging as the primary payment method across completed rides.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used:
SQL: Data extraction, joins, aggregate queries, and data validation.
Power Query: Data cleaning, type transformations, custom columns, and handling missing/null values across trip metrics.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Calculated key KPIs including Completion Rates, Dynamic Measures, VTAT/CTAT averages, and conditional aggregations.
Power BI: Data modeling, interactive slicers, decomposition trees, and customized multi-page report canvas design.

💡 What I Learned: This project gave me hands-on experience in handling real-world operational edge cases—such as treating null values correctly during averaging to prevent skewed performance metrics—and structuring dashboards for operational decision-makers.
